The Reality Check
Before we begin, take a moment to consider: 1 in how many children face food insecurity? Make your guess. The reality might surprise you.
1 in 5 Children Go Hungry
The answer is 1 in 5 children, which is over 13 million kids across America who don't know where their next meal is coming from. Behind this statistic are real children facing impossible circumstances every day.

Let's explore where and why this is happening.
Children Change Everything
Food insecurity doesn't affect every community equally. Across the U.S., the presence of children in a household dramatically changes the equation. Explore this map to see how food insecurity rates shift when we compare households with and without children.
A System Out of Balance
Zooming out to the national picture, another truth becomes clear:

Child hunger is not just widespread, it's uneven. Some states have made progress. Others have fallen further behind. What you'll see next is that the scale doesn't tip the way you might expect. Explore the Lady Justice scale to see how much child participation in the National School Lunch Program shifted over the past year, and which states were lifted up or left behind.
Same visualization multiple sections
Some visualizations may be relevant to multiple sections. You can reuse the same visualization across different sections to reinforce key points. Or build on the previous visualization to just add on.
Examples
